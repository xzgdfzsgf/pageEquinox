# Prompts para imágenes con IA — EQUINOX

Estilo común (añadir a cada prompt):
> Cinematic, photorealistic, deep navy night palette (#0E1540, #0A0F33) with soft lilac (#E9C4F4), violet (#A979D9)
> and subtle pink (#EC5FAA) light accents, clean composition with negative space on the left for text, no text,
> no logos, no people's faces in close-up, 16:9.

Guardar en `public/img/` con el nombre indicado. La web las usa automáticamente.

## hero.jpg — Hero (fondo, 2400×1400)
Earth seen from low orbit at night over the equator, a thin glowing line of the equator crossing the planet,
satellites in orbit leaving faint luminous arcs, starfield, Andes mountains faintly visible near Ecuador,
atmosphere glow in lilac and violet. Dark on the left third for headline legibility.

## conference.jpg — The Conference, UDLA Park (1600×1000)
Modern conference auditorium in Quito prepared for an international space economy summit, large stage with
a wide LED screen showing an abstract orbital graphic, navy and lilac stage lighting, audience silhouettes from
behind, premium and institutional atmosphere. *(Si hay fotos reales de UDLA Park en la presentación, usar esas.)*

## stargate.jpg — The Stargate Inauguration, Urcuquí (1600×1000)
Large satellite ground station antennas (teleport) at dusk in the green Andean highlands of Urcuquí, Ecuador,
equator marker monument nearby, dramatic twilight sky turning navy with first stars, lilac rim light on the
dishes. *(Priorizar el material real de Astralintu/Stargate de la presentación.)*

## og.jpg — Imagen para compartir en redes (1200×630)
Same as hero but centered composition with generous empty space in the middle for overlaying the EQUINOX logo
afterwards (el logo se agrega en Canva/Figma, no con la IA).
